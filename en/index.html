What is an independent wedding producer?

An independent wedding producer (in Hebrew, mafik chatunot chitzoni) is a professional who does not work for the venue. They work only for the couple: choosing the venue and vendors, negotiating prices, building the budget and the evening’s schedule, and running the wedding on the day.

FAQ

What everyone
asks me.

Why hire an independent producer if the venue has an event manager?

The venue’s event manager works for the venue. An independent producer works only for you: he knows the vendors, compares quotes, negotiates and protects your budget, and on the day makes sure everything happens on time and exactly as planned.

How long before the wedding should we get in touch?

As early as you can. I usually accompany couples for five to ten months before the wedding. That leaves time to choose the venue and vendors calmly and to secure the best terms.

How do you help us save money?

I know what you really need and what you don’t, and I sit across from the vendors for you. I won’t let you pay for things you don’t need: we compare offers, drop what doesn’t matter to you and invest in what does.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope of the planning and the kind of event. At our first meeting we work out what you need, and you get a clear proposal with no small print.

Can you plan a wedding in Israel if we live abroad?

Yes. Much of the work happens over WhatsApp and video calls, and I handle the venue visits and vendor meetings on the ground. You arrive for a wedding that is ready.
